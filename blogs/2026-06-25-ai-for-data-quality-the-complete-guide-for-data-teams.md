This guide explains how AI can automate data quality management at scale through assistive and agentic modes, both relying on data contracts as executable specifications. It argues manual checks no longer suffice given data volume and AI consumption, and shows how tools like Soda AI generate and enforce contracts that humans and AI agents can trust.
